What if a design could remember how it evolved?

MEMO Brain is our research direction for connected design knowledge: project geometry, constraints, choices, versions and feedback in one explainable workspace.

The vision is straightforward: don't lose the reasoning behind a design. Make it accessible, editable and able to grow with the project.
