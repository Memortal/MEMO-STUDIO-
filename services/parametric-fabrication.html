Digital Fabrication & Public Art

Develop material-aware patterns, surfaces and components for fabrication, installations, feature walls, sculptural projects and products.

Problems this service addresses

  • Parametric façades and custom perforated or folded panels
  • CNC-ready components, nesting and fabrication geometry
  • Public-art concepts, installations and product systems

Typical deliverables

  • Rationalized geometry and component breakdown
  • Fabrication files and annotated assembly concepts
  • Design studies, renders and specifications for review

Who this is for

Fabricators, public-art teams, architects, manufacturers, exhibition studios.

Start with a defined problem, sample inputs and success criteria. The technical scope, handoff and validation are agreed before work begins.
