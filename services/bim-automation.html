BIM Automation & Coordination

Automate documentation, model checks, structured data and coordination workflows so your team can work faster with fewer manual steps.

Problems this service addresses

  • Repetitive tasks in Revit or Dynamo
  • IFC model inspection, classification and openBIM handoffs
  • QA/QC reporting, element schedules and drawing workflows

Typical deliverables

  • Custom scripts and tested sample workflow
  • IFC/model data viewer or audit report
  • Documented inputs, source code and team handoff

Who this is for

Architects, VDC teams, contractors, BIM managers, engineering teams.

Start with a defined problem, sample inputs and success criteria. The technical scope, handoff and validation are agreed before work begins.
